The template. Treating a root as a fundamental, its partials land (rounded to equal temperament) on the unison, the fifth, the major third, the minor seventh, the major second, and so on. Stacking partial amplitudes (1/n) by pitch class gives the weight bars above, a fixed prior on which notes a root "wants" to generate.

The minor third is missing. No low partial lands on it, so it earns almost no support. A minor triad's root therefore scores barely above its relative major, the model is genuinely unsure who the root is, which is the same ambiguity that gives minor its softer, less-anchored footing.
